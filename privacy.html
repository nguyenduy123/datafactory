Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Datafactory is a private, internal tool. It is used only by its owner, with the Google accounts of the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app accesses the following data of the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • Channel and video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports.
  • The list of published videos and their titles and descriptions.
  • Comments on the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner how their channels perform and to help them plan and review their videos.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is not sold, and is not shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • The app does not collect data from any other person.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner on their computer.
