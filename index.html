Datafactory

Datafactory is a private, internal tool used by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

What the app does

  • Reads statistics and analytics of the owner's own YouTube channels (views, watch time, published videos).
  • Reads comments on the owner's own videos so the owner can reply to them.
  • Stores this data only on the owner's own computer, to plan and review their videos.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the app, signing in with the Google accounts of their own channels.
